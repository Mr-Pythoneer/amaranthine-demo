## Processing

- Each photo is cropped. People photos are about 4:5 portrait with the face placed in the upper third.
- Photos are resized to at most 1200px on the long side and saved as JPEG at quality 78 with all metadata (EXIF/ICC/XMP) removed. Each file is under 150KB.
- **wedding-1969.jpg** is cropped below the chapel's sign, so no signage is visible.
- **walter-2.jpg** and **cabin-1974.jpg** are cropped inside the black slide-mount edges.
- No retouching, colour grading or compositing was done. The faded colour of the vintage photos is in the original scans.
